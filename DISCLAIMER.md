## ⚖️ 1. Legal Framework, Asset Ingestion, and Fair Use Protection

This repository is structurally organized to operate under the protective statutory boundaries of **Section 107 of the United States Copyright Act (Fair Use Guidelines)**. 

Data preservation logged within this matrix is maintained strictly for the purposes of:
- **Public-Interest Criticism and Commentary**
